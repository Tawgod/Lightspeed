# Product Catalog M2 — internal typed identity evidence (draft)
Single-tenant trusted offline fixtures only. Query: kind, exactValue and supplierId for SUPPLIER_SKU or manufacturerId for MANUFACTURER_PART. Records: productId, variantId (explicit null for nonvariant), active (boolean or null), identifiers (typed values with source/provenance). IDs and values are literal nonempty strings. No UPC/GTIN equivalence or case/space/punctuation normalization; no vendor field shape is assumed. A variant and its parent are separate targets; inactive matches remain collisions. Coverage defaults false and must be proven independently for the exact scope, including inactive and variants. Duplicate observations merge provenance; conflicting activity makes coverage incomplete. Results are evidence only, never permission or a create decision. Supplier IDs are canonical inputs, not inferred aliases; primary/secondary mappings and cost are deferred. Purchasing handoff assigns canonical supplier-product identity to Catalog; shared public schema still requires manager/Purchasing review. Legacy matcher and all readers unchanged.
