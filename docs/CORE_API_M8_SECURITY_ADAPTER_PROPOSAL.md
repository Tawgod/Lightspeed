## Contract proposal (Security to confirm or replace exact names/encoding)
Version candidate HC-AUTHZ/0.1 synthetic only. Trusted injected Security boundary resolves opaque actor evidence, never browser-supplied actor/role/session/permission claims. Resolve canonical realm/id/tenant/environment, session/device/assurance reference or service/job/revision/audience/delegation binding. Keep proof references and policy epoch out of immutable execution digest; immutable actor and delegation/job identity must be bound. No untrusted principal fallback, tool-derived grants, Owner bypass, alias substitution or raw credentials. Core never determines whether a PIN, strong proof, role, restriction or delegated scope is sufficient.

Request candidate fields: version, unique decision requestId, correlationId, stage, actor binding, executionDigest, canonical complete executionEffects, stage-specific accessEffects, original approval reference/proof binding, evidence revision, expected policy freshness context. Every effect includes exact operation, tenant, environment, location, typed resource identity, field and domain-defined typed revision/quantity/amount/currency/relationships where applicable; access also purpose/audience and exact receipt fields. Security defines canonical serialization and binding/digest algorithms; no duplicate canonicalizer/evaluator. Domain derives effects from trusted intent and locked/fresh records, not user claims. Reject incomplete or unmappable scopes, do not encode variant/supplier IDs or money in lossy strings. Access to related targets remains denied until agreed derivation. All effects must be authorized together; no mixing partial grants or independent decision responses across policy epochs.

Response candidate fields: version, requestId, exact decisionBinding, executionDigest, actor binding, stage, policyVersion, issuedAt, expiresAt, allowed (strict boolean); no policy explanation or secrets passed to ordinary callers. Security specifies exact required/optional fields, trusted clock, validity interval, freshness/version read or epoch contract, and binding verification method. Core validates response shape, version, correlation/binding, expected stage/actor/tenant/digest, current epoch and interval, rejects malformed/unavailable/timeout/unknown/stale/revoked results and maps only a validated allow to literal true. No cached allow across stages or permissive fallback; separate internal audit correlation and sanitized outward denial. A fresh check is not a distributed revocation fence; actual atomicity remains gated. Security must specify whether freshness check is a separate trusted method or integrated decision contract. Do not implement a made-up signature protocol.

## M7 compatibility and composition
M7 local currently passes per-effect authorizeExecution, separate verifyApproval and authorizeAccess callbacks; execution digest prepared independently of access/approval, persisted proof digest, flat receipt allowlist, employee-only, related-target and nonempty multi-target projection gated. Remote M7 has different actor/effect shape, typed exact reservation tuples, current checks before reserve/dispatch/readback/reconciliation/projection, unknown holds; no local transaction around dispatch. Propose additive M8 consumer factory and trusted wrapper for each M7 path, not editing existing exports or reinterpreting persisted M7 digests. Full decision request must bind entire effect set, not just one callback effect. Need agreed stage mapping and whole-set snapshot context; existing M7 callbacks lack a shared current epoch and some access context. Do not silently adapt by issuing unrelated per-effect decisions or claiming atomic revocation. Prefer additive orchestration context/version or wrapper agreed by Security and Manager before executable changes. Core retains receipt filtering and state machine; Security owns policy/approval truth. Denied or indeterminate disclosure must not imply rollback, leak receipt/existence or trigger redispatch. Unknown remote reservations remain held across keys. Independent local receipt inspection is not remote PO reconciliation or retry authority.

## Assurance and lifecycle
S25G accepted fixture distinguishes active canonical employee session/device/site assurance, on-site PIN without repeated factors for ordinary authorized actions, off-site strong proof, sensitive remote 30-minute non-sliding freshness and admin strong proof with unapproved max. Service proof bound to credential revision/audience/validity, job identity/revision, delegated employee current authority intersected with service/job/delegation and restrictions; standing service explicitly approved and accountable, no borrowed human session. S25G has separate assurance and service evaluator shapes, not a unified consumer protocol; its synthetic clock equality and policy version checks do not establish production timing. Security S2.5H to compose and own exact employee/service contract. M7 rejects service actors today; M8 must preserve that behavior unless explicitly approved additive namespace/actor binding supports service. No actual role/alias changes; all 19 legacy directions provisional. Ordinary authorized employee wholesale cost/customer access preserved; master and PO line cost writes independent.
